Wedding Weekend Is Here!

We’re so happy you made it. Before the celebrations begin, here are the most important things to know for the weekend.

Save this page for quick reminders, schedules, and wedding-day details.

Shuttle Transportation — Nakoma ↔ Chalet View

Complimentary shuttle transportation will be available for guests staying at Nakoma, providing transportation from Nakoma to Chalet View for the wedding ceremony and reception, and back to Nakoma afterward.

We anticipate the shuttle departing Nakoma at approximately 4:00 PM, with return transportation from Chalet View to Nakoma around 12:00 AM. Final shuttle times and pickup details will be shared with guests.

There is no curfew! If you’d like to keep celebrating at Chalet View after the scheduled return shuttle, you’re more than welcome to stay — just plan to arrange your own transportation back to Nakoma.

Everyone Is Invited

All wedding weekend festivities are for all of our guests, regardless of where you’re staying. Whether you’re at one of our recommended hotels, an Airbnb, with family or friends, or somewhere else entirely, we’d love for you to join us for everything planned throughout the weekend.

Important

Bring cash for the Money Dance

We’ll be celebrating with a traditional Money Dance at the reception. Please bring cash if you’d like to join in and dance with the newlyweds.

Our formal black dress code applies only to the wedding ceremony and reception. There is no specific dress code for the other wedding weekend events, so come comfortable and ready to celebrate! The ceremony and reception are outdoors, so choose grass-friendly shoes too.
